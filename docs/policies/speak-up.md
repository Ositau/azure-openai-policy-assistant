# Speak Up (Whistleblowing) Policy

Kestrel Bridge Finance is a fictional company. This policy is synthetic demo content.

## What to report

Report any concern about wrongdoing, such as fraud, bribery, breaches of regulation, risks to customers, or covering up any of these.

## How to raise a concern

- Speak to your line manager, or if that is not appropriate, to the Whistleblowing Champion, who is a non-executive director.
- You can report anonymously through the independent Speak Up line, by phone or online, 24 hours a day.
- You can also report directly to the financial regulators.

## Protection

- We will not tolerate any retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith. Retaliation is a disciplinary offence.
- Your identity will be kept confidential and shared only with those who need to know to investigate.

## What happens next

The Whistleblowing Champion oversees the investigation. Where possible, you will be told when the investigation is closed, though not always the outcome.
