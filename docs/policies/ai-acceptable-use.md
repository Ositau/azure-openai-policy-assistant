# AI Acceptable Use Policy

Kestrel Bridge Finance is a fictional company. This policy is synthetic demo content.

## Approved tools

- The approved AI tools are the internal Policy Assistant and Microsoft 365 Copilot with your company account.
- Public AI tools (such as free consumer chatbots) may be used only with public information. Never enter customer data, colleague data or confidential company information into them.

## Using AI output

- You are responsible for anything you send or decide, even if AI helped produce it.
- AI output must be checked by a person before it is sent to a customer.
- AI must not be used to make or automate decisions about credit, lending, pricing for an individual customer, or employment. These decisions need a human decision-maker.

## Records

If AI materially contributed to advice or a customer communication, note this in the customer record.

## Incidents

Report any AI incident, such as confidential data entered into a public tool or a harmful or incorrect output reaching a customer, to the AI Governance Lead and the IT Service Desk on the same day.
