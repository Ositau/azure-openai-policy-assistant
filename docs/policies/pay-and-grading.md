# Pay Bands and Annual Pay Review

Kestrel Bridge Finance is a fictional company. This document is synthetic demo content. CONFIDENTIAL: People Team only.

## Pay bands 2026

- Grade 1: £24,000 to £29,000
- Grade 2: £29,000 to £36,000
- Grade 3: £36,000 to £47,000
- Grade 4: £47,000 to £62,000
- Grade 5: £62,000 to £85,000
- Grade 6 and above: set by the Remuneration Committee

## Annual pay review

- Pay reviews take effect on 1 April each year.
- The overall budget is agreed by the Remuneration Committee in February.
- Increases above 8% for an individual need approval from the People Director.
- Staff on a formal performance improvement plan are not eligible for an increase that year.

## New starters

New starters are normally appointed in the lower half of their grade's band. Offers above the band midpoint need People Director approval.
