# Gifts and Hospitality Policy

Kestrel Bridge Finance is a fictional company. This policy is synthetic demo content.

## Purpose

We must never give or receive anything that could influence, or look like it influences, a business decision. This policy explains what you may accept or offer, who must approve it and how to record it.

## Gifts you receive

- Gifts worth £50 or less may be accepted without approval, provided they are not cash or cash equivalents.
- Gifts worth more than £50 and up to £150 need approval from your line manager before you accept them.
- Gifts worth more than £150 need approval from Compliance. These are normally declined.
- If you are unsure of the value, assume it is above the threshold and ask.

## Gifts that are never allowed

- Cash, vouchers, gift cards, cryptocurrency or anything that can be converted to cash, whatever the amount.
- Any gift or hospitality from a supplier taking part in an open tender or contract renewal with us.
- Any gift offered in return for a specific decision or outcome.

## Hospitality

- Business meals with clients or suppliers are acceptable where there is a clear business purpose and the cost is reasonable, normally no more than £100 per person.
- Tickets to sporting or cultural events need line manager approval, and the host must attend.
- Travel or accommodation paid for by a third party always needs Compliance approval.

## Recording

All gifts and hospitality worth more than £25, whether accepted or declined, must be recorded in the Gifts and Hospitality Register within 5 working days.

## Breaches

Failure to follow this policy may lead to disciplinary action. Report concerns to Compliance or through the Speak Up process.
