# Information Security Standard

Kestrel Bridge Finance is a fictional company. This standard is synthetic demo content.

## Passwords and sign-in

- Passwords must be at least 14 characters. A passphrase of four or more random words is recommended.
- Multi-factor authentication is required for all company systems.
- Never share your password or approve an MFA prompt you did not start.

## Devices

- Lock your screen whenever you leave your device (Windows key + L).
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk immediately.
- Do not install software that is not in the Company Portal.

## Email and phishing

- Report suspicious emails with the Report button in Outlook. Do not forward them to colleagues.
- Never click links or open attachments in unexpected emails asking you to sign in or make payments.

## Clean desk

Clear confidential papers from your desk at the end of each day and lock them away.

## Visitors

Visitors must sign in at reception, wear a visitor badge and be accompanied at all times.
