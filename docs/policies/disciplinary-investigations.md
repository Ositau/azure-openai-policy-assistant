# Disciplinary Investigations Guide

Kestrel Bridge Finance is a fictional company. This document is synthetic demo content. CONFIDENTIAL: People Team only.

## Appointing an investigator

The investigating manager must not be the employee's line manager and must have no involvement in the matter.

## Suspension

- Suspension on full pay may be used only where there is a risk to evidence, to customers or colleagues, or where the allegation is gross misconduct.
- Suspension is not a disciplinary sanction and must be reviewed every 2 weeks.
- A People Partner must approve any suspension before it is communicated.

## Investigation meetings

- Give the employee at least 48 hours' written notice of an investigation meeting.
- Employees may be accompanied by a colleague or trade union representative at disciplinary hearings.
- Aim to complete investigations within 4 weeks.

## Records

Investigation files are kept for 12 months after the case closes if no action is taken, and for the duration of any warning plus 12 months otherwise.
