# Complaints Handling Procedure

Kestrel Bridge Finance is a fictional company. This procedure is synthetic demo content.

## What is a complaint

Any expression of dissatisfaction from a customer, spoken or written, about our products or service, whether or not they use the word "complaint".

## Logging

- Log every complaint in the complaints system within 1 working day of receiving it.
- Record what the customer is unhappy about and what they want us to do.

## Timescales

- If the complaint is fully resolved by the end of the third business day after it was received, send the customer a summary resolution letter.
- Otherwise, send a written acknowledgement within 5 working days.
- We must send a final response within 8 weeks of receiving the complaint.

## Final response

- The final response must explain our decision and any redress.
- It must tell the customer they can refer the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service within 6 months, and include the leaflet.

## Escalation

Complaints involving a vulnerable customer, alleged fraud, or a potential regulatory breach must be escalated to the Head of Customer Operations on the same day.
