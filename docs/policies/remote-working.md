# Hybrid and Remote Working Policy

Kestrel Bridge Finance is a fictional company. This policy is synthetic demo content.

## Hybrid pattern

- Staff may work remotely for up to 3 days per week, agreed with their line manager.
- Teams may set anchor days when everyone is expected in the office.
- Core hours are 10am to 4pm, when you should be available for meetings.

## Working from abroad

- Working from outside the UK is allowed for a maximum of 10 working days in a calendar year.
- You need approval from both the People Team and IT Security at least 2 weeks before you travel, because of tax, regulatory and data-transfer rules.
- Working from countries on the IT Security restricted list is not allowed.

## Security at home

- Use a private, password-protected home network. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the company VPN connected.
- Do not print customer documents at home.
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your device, even at home.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, monitor and headset. Report faults to the IT Service Desk. Return all equipment when you leave the company.
