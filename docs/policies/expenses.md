# Travel and Expenses Policy

Kestrel Bridge Finance is a fictional company. This policy is synthetic demo content.

## Principles

Spend company money as if it were your own. Claim only costs you incurred wholly for business.

## Travel

- Rail travel is standard class. First class is only allowed for journeys over 3 hours, with line manager approval in advance.
- Mileage in your own car is paid at 45p per mile for the first 10,000 business miles in a tax year and 25p per mile after that.
- Taxis are allowed when public transport is not practical, or between 10pm and 6am.

## Hotels

- Hotel stays are capped at £180 per night in London and £120 per night elsewhere in the UK, including breakfast.
- Book through the company travel portal wherever possible.

## Meals

- When travelling overnight on business, meals are reimbursed up to £30 per day.
- Alcohol is not reimbursed, except when entertaining clients with line manager approval in advance. Alcohol at internal team events is never reimbursed.

## Making a claim

- Submit claims within 30 days of the cost being incurred. Claims older than 90 days will not be paid.
- Itemised receipts are required for every item over £10.
- Your line manager approves your claim; Finance may audit any claim.

## Corporate card

Corporate cards must only be used for business expenses. Personal use, even if repaid, is a breach of this policy.
